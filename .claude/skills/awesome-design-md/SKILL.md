# Awesome DESIGN.md (reference library)

Each folder in `designs/<site>/` holds a `DESIGN.md` (the analysed design system) and a `README.md`.
List them with `ls designs/`.

## How to use

1. Shortlist 2–4 sites whose feel fits the brief (e.g. for a premium aviation app: `spacex`, `dji`,
   `linear.app`, `raycast`, `apple`, `tesla`) and read their `DESIGN.md`.
2. Take principles, not identity: type scale ratios, spacing rhythm, contrast, how they treat
   product imagery, motion restraint. Blend them into the project's own brand.
3. Never copy a brand's identity: no logos, names, signature colour combinations or proprietary
   typefaces. The result must look like the project's own brand, not an imitation of the source.
4. Fonts named in a DESIGN.md may be proprietary or served from third parties. Use licensed fonts and
   self-host them when the project requires it (blades-app.com: no third-party requests, see
   `website/privacy.html` §11).
