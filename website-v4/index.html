Built for how you fly.

One app, four kinds of flying. Each gets the fields it needs.

Student & PPL

  • Dual and PIC time, and who you flew with
  • Passenger currency by day and by night
  • Hours towards your next licence

Airline

  • Multi-pilot, SIC and pilot flying or monitoring
  • Log from a photo of the ACARS flight log
  • Approaches, holds and simulator sessions (FFS, FTD, FNPT)

Helicopter

  • Single- and multi-engine, single- and multi-pilot
  • NVG time, hot refuels and touch-and-gos
  • Night worked out along the route

Drone

  • A1, A2, A3, Specific and Certified, from the C0–C6 class mark
  • VLOS, EVLOS and BVLOS time, and roles like RPIC and observer
  • 90-day recency per category, and a warning above 120 m
