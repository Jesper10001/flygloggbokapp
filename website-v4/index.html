    Questions

    Do I need an account?

    No. There is no sign-up. Everything works on your iPhone from the first launch.

    Where is my logbook stored?

    On your iPhone. We keep no copy of it and cannot see it. You can turn on AES-256 encryption in Settings, and back up to your own iCloud.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. Logging, the logbook and exports work without a connection. Weather, maps and the AI scan need one.

    Which logbook layouts are there?

    EASA AMC1 FCL.050, the Swedish EASA logbook, a professional pilot logbook, FAA 14 CFR 61.51 and a remote pilot logbook for drones.

    Can I bring my old logbook?

    Yes. Import a CSV, or enter your totals by hand. You can export a PDF or a CSV of everything at any time.

    How do I cancel Premium?

    Apple manages the subscription. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
