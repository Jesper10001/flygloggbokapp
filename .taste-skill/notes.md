# BLADES v4 — notes

## Brief (reading, one line)
A new, shorter blades-app.com: a native iOS logbook for helicopter, aeroplane and drone pilots. One job for the page: get a pilot to download it (App Store; free to start).

## Inventory (belongs to BLADES, no competitor)
1. The name: rotor BLADES (helicopter origin; AS350 / AW109, HEMS) and the B-mark with blades.
2. The paper logbook spread: EASA columns, "Total this page / Brought forward / Total to date", "Certified true and correct", pilot's signature, page numbers 55–56.
3. The ritual: shutdown → log on the ramp → open the page → copy the rows across → sign.
4. Zulu time: 12:25 Z, block off/on, UTC everywhere.
5. Swedish ICAO codes: ESSB Bromma, ESGG Landvetter, ESOW Västerås, ESSA Arlanda. Built in Sweden.
6. Night from civil twilight at the departure field — computed, not typed.
7. The licence ladder: ATPL(A) 1500 h, FCL.510.A, PIC 250 h, night 100 h.
8. Instrument/ACARS scan: photograph the flight log, the fields fill themselves.
9. The airport map at night: every field you have touched, city lights.
10. Drones: A1/A2/A3, VLOS/EVLOS/BVLOS, a fleet of airframes.
11. Offline, no account; data on the phone; CSV export; optional AES-256.
12. Price: free for 20 flights with everything; Premium 39 kr/month.
13. Colours: night-navy #0A1628, instrument cyan #00C8E8, warning gold #FFB830, paper cream of the logbook.

## Argument (questions)
- Reader: a working or training pilot (heli/fixed-wing), also drone operators. Knows paper logbooks and that adding columns is tedious; has tried a logbook app or a spreadsheet.
- What it is, to a friend: "An app where you log the flight in seconds after shutdown, and it shows you your paper logbook page already filled in, totals carried forward, so you just copy it across."
- Believe after one screen: logging is seconds, and the paper book stops being maths.
- Proof: the real app screen + the real logbook spread.
- Next: download on the App Store (free).
