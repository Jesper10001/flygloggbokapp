ESSV 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1 Availability 
 RWY 10/28 and TWY G are available during MAY–SEP.
 During other period information on runway conditions shall be obtained from ATS for flight planning.
 2 Start-up and clearance for IFR traffic 
 Startup and clearance shall be requested on channel 120.305 not earlier than 30 MIN before EOBT.
 3 Start-up and clearance for VFR traffic 
 Start-up and clearance shall be requested before taxiing from apron B.
 4 School and training flights 
 For school and training, permission is required. Permission by ATS TEL +46 498 26 31 42.
 5 Parachuting 
 For parachuting, permission is required.
 Permission by ATS.
 Parachuting landing area – see ESSV Aerodrome Chart.
 6 Parking Apron A 
 Prior permission required (PPR) for non-schedule flights for parking apron A, except ambulance flight. Maximum parking time is 60 minutes unless otherwise agreed. Request shall be addressed to 
 vby.ado@swedavia.se or TEL +46 10 109 52 20.
 7 Ground movement procedures 
 Engines shall be operated at minimum power required when taxiing on apron A and B. Caution advised when turning around on apron A and B. Watch out for passengers on aprons. Oversteering is required when taxiing from/to south TWY M from/to apron A stand 1, 2 and 3. 
Code C aircraft are requested to avoid executing 180-degree turns on the runway threshold.
 8 Engine ground run 
 Engine ground run shall take place 0500-2100 (0400-2000). Engine ground run (high power) shall be performed in the manoeuvering area. Engine ground run (idle power) on Apron A.
 If, for flight-safety reasons, engine ground run must take place 2100–0500 (2000-0400), the following applies:
 • Engine ground run shall be performed on Apron A.
 • A report shall be sent to ADO: 
 vby.ado@swedavia.se . The report must state when the engine ground run started and ended.
