ESCM 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. Right hand traffic circuit to RWY 21 and RWY 26.
 2. Outside ATS hours of operation monitoring and intention shall be transmitted on channel 119.205 when flying within the boundaries of Uppsala CTR. Except for traffic in, or in the vicinity of ESKC traffic circuit, where ESKC channel 123.350 shall be used.
 3. Neither airwork, approaches nor landings are permitted without special permission. Generally, only flights important to society will be granted such permission.
 4. RWY guard lights (RGL) are activated during ATS hours of operation.
