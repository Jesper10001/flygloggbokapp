ESKM 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. For VFR landing outside ATS hours the intention to land and ETA shall be clearly declared on ESKM ATS channel (119.055 MHz) followed by a RWY crossing (500 ft + traffic pattern altitude) in order to verify RWY availability and alerting AD personnel.
 2. For take-off outside ATS hours the intention to takeoff and ETD shall be clearly declared on ESKM ATS channel 119.055 MHz.
