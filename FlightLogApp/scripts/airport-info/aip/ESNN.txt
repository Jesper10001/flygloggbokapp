ESNN 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. Start-up clearance shall be obtained from TWR at all times
 2. When flight safety, traffic situation and weather conditions permit, landings arriving from the south must be performed on RWY 34 and take-offs for destinations to the south must be performed on RWY 16.
 3. Aircraft parked on stands 2-6 or 11-16 shall not perform power pushback as a standard procedure. This is only permitted if the aerodrome is unable to perform pushback by towing or TBL tractor.
