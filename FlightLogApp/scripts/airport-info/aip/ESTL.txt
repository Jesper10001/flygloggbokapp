ESTL 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. Right hand traffic circuit when RWY 29L/R is in use.
 2. Air traffic outside ATS hours of operation:
 Aerodrome consent is required for all traffic.
 Take-off or landing may only take place when visibility exceeds 800 m.
 No traffic may take place during twilight and darkness.
 All traffic within the CTR must transmit their intention on channel 130.705.
 Ground vehicles can occur without two-way VHF communication.
