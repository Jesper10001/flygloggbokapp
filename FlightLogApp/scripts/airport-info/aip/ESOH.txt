ESOH 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. ESOH IAC: NDB RWY 36 and AB holding is available with prior permission from ESOK ATS.
 2. For landing outside ATS operational hours intention to land and time to landing shall be clearly declared on channel for Hagfors AFIS, followed by a RWY crossing at AD traffic pattern altitude +500 ft in order to verify RWY availability and to alert AD personnel of presence.
 3. For take-off outside ATS operational hours intention to start and time to start shall be clearly declared on channel for Hagfors AFIS.
