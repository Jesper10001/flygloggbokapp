ESNX 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. PPR by school and training flights during hours of TWR/AFIS. PPR TEL +46 960 173 85.
 2. Right hand traffic circuit applies to RWY 30 during take-off and landing exercises outside hours of TWR.
 3. Holding on TWY A is not available during AFIS hours and when RWY is blocked by other ACFT.
 4. Start-up request with ATS is mandatory for all aircraft. Traffic using ground handling services must always coordinate start-up with marshaller/ground crew, prior to this request.
