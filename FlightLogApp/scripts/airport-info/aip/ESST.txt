ESST 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. School and training flights with repeated TKOF and LDG not permitted between 2100-0500 (2000-0400).
 2. When ATS is closed, a visual check of the runway shall be conducted before take off to ensure that there are no other activities or vehicles occupying the movement area. 
Before landing, a “visual fly over check” shall be conducted to ensure that there are no other activities or vehicles occupying the movement area.
