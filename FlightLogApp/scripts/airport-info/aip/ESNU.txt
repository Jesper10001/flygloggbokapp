ESNU 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. Daily between 2100–0600 (2000–0500) the aerodrome must not be used by aircraft certificated in accordance with ICAO Annex 16, Volume I, Part II, Chapter 2.
 2. Start-up and clearance for IFR-traffic shall be requested from ATC on channel 119.805. For IFR-traffic shall request not be made earlier than 30 min before EOBT.
 3. During the hours 2100–0600 (2000–0500) take-off RWY 32 and landing RWY 14 not permitted unless otherwise required by flight safety reasons.
 4. During the hours 2100–0600 (2000–0500) engine reverse should be avoided.
 5. Ground movement procedures
 Engines shall be operated at minimum power required when taxiing on apron. Caution advised when turning around on apron. Watch out for passengers on apron.
 6. APU
 APU shall not be used on parking unless required for engine start or adjustment of cabin heat. On these occasions APU must not be started earlier than 5 min before estimated time for taxiing.
 When the temperature outside exceeds 25°C and where air cannot otherwise be circulated in the cabin, APU may be started at a maximum of 20 min before estimated time for taxing.
