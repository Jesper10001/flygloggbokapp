ESGJ 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1. Outside ATS operational hours, flights are conducted in accordance with SERA.
 2. Outside ATS operational hours, blind transmission is mandatory within the geographical area of the established ESGJ CTR. The intention to take off or land must be clearly announced on channel 118.255. If the runway is not available in its full length and width, take-off or landing should not be performed.
 3. Vehicle traffic without two-way radio communication may occur in the maneuvering area outside ATS operational hours.
