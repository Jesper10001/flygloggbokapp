ESMK 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1 General 
 Aircraft having a MTOM exceeding 5700 kg shall, on take-off RWY 19, climb straight ahead to 1500 ft until turn is initiated. For traffic with MTOM 5700 kg or below, right hand circuit applies after departure when RWY 19 is in use.
 2 Use of APU 
 Arrival
 APU shall be shut down soonest after parking. 
Departure
 APU shall not be started until OK from ground crew. When the outside temperature exceeds 25°C, APU may be started not earlier than 10 min before estimated time for taxiing.
 3 Parking outside ATS operational hours 
 Parking on apron outside ATS operational hours is not allowed. Exceptions for SAR, ambulance and police.
 4 Vehicle traffic on manoeuvring area 
 Vehicle traffic may occur on the manoeuvring area outside ATS operational hours.
 5 Routines outside ATS opening hours 
 Outside ATS operational hours, blind transmission is mandatory in ESMK CTR. The intention to take off or land must be clearly announced on channel 129.355.
