ESNK 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS 
 1 Landing outside ATS operational hours 
 For landing outside ATS operational hours intention to land and time to landing shall be clearly declared on channel for Kramfors AFIS, followed by a RWY crossing at AD traffic pattern altitude + 500 ft in order to verify RWY availability and to alert AD personnel of presence.
 2 Take-off outside ATS operational hours 
 For take-off outside ATS operational hours intention to start and time to start shall be clearly declared on channel for Kramfors AFIS.
