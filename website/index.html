Why it exists

I built it for myself

I fly for a living. After every flight I wanted two things: to get it logged in seconds, and to copy it into my paper logbook without adding up columns by hand. No app did both, so I built one.

BLADES is the logbook I use myself, made exactly the way I want it. Every feature in it is something I needed on my own flights.
